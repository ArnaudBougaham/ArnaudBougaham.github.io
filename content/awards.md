## Awards & Recognition

### 2025

**🏆 First Prize - PINKCC Challenge 2025**
- **Date**: June 13, 2025
- **Description**: Awarded first place in the PINKCC Data Challenge, which focused on revolutionizing ovarian cancer research using AI applications. This recognition highlights expertise in applying machine learning and generative models to critical medical research problems.
- **Impact**: Demonstrated the effectiveness of quality-constrained anomaly detection methods in medical imaging applications.

**🏆 Factory of the Future Award**
- **Organization**: AISIN Europe (formerly AW Europe)
- **Years**: 2019, 2022
- **Description**: Recognition for pivotal contributions to the digital transformation of the production center of AISIN Europe. The award acknowledges innovative implementation of Industry 4.0 technologies, including AI-driven quality control systems and operational technology solutions.
- **Role**: As Operational Technologies Engineer, played a key role in developing and implementing digital transformation initiatives that led to this recognition.
